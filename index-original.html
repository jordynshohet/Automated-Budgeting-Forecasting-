Model Assumptions

  • All data and costs have been populated through March. The model projects costs for April through December.
  • All resources have 160 hours total per month.
  • FTE resources take a maximum of 160 hours of PTO during the year.
  • Contractor resources take a maximum of 120 hours of PTO during the year.
  • PTO floor: All employees are assumed to take at least some PTO. FTEs forecast a minimum of 12 hrs/month base (before seasonal weighting), contractors 8 hrs/month.
  • Roster does not fluctuate (no hires, no attrition).
  • Projects P00010 (WiFi Connectivity) and P00014 (CPE Enhancements) end after August. Freed hours are reallocated to other active projects or General OpEx.
  • Total AWS spend shall not exceed $2.1M for the year.
  • AWS accounts ending in 8, 11, and 27 grow 5% month over month from March.
  • All remaining AWS accounts use their YTD weighted average (3-month avg) for projections.
  • Seasonality applied: Work hours reduced in summer (Jun 90%, Jul 85%, Aug 90%) and holidays (Nov 90%, Dec 80%).
  • AWS seasonality: Cloud costs reduced slightly in summer (93-95%) and December (90%).

Forecasting Methodology

Resource Forecasting: For each employee-project combination, we calculate the 3-month average (Jan-Mar) and apply a +/- 10 hour variance for each forecasted month, multiplied by a seasonality factor. When projects end (P00010, P00014 after August), freed capacity is redistributed proportionally.

AWS Forecasting: Growth accounts compound at 5% monthly from their March baseline. All other accounts use their 3-month average with a +/- $300 variance and a seasonal multiplier. The model validates against the $2.1M annual cap.

Organization Story

This business unit operates across 20 projects with 30 employees (20 FTEs, 10 contractors) spanning 7 locations. The portfolio is weighted toward Capital Expense work with a healthy Operating Expense base for maintenance and support.
